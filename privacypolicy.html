Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

ApnaChamber is run by Rishab Kanaujia ("we"). We build and maintain websites for advocates and law firms. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how long we keep it.

What we collect

When you fill in an enquiry form or message us on WhatsApp, we receive the details you choose to share: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, city or court, practice area, an optional LinkedIn profile, and any notes you write.

If you become a client, we also keep the details needed to build your website (your biography, enrolment details, office address and photographs) and your billing records.

Why we use it

We use your information only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a sample or quotation, build and deliver your website, and meet our accounting and legal duties. We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not add you to any marketing list.

Who else sees it

Enquiry forms are processed by Formspree (formspree.io) and sent to our email inbox. Domain registration, hosting and email are provided by other companies. When your project is complete, these are registered in your own name. We share your details with these providers only as much as needed to deliver the service.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept while we work together, plus the period required by tax and accounting rules.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Write to contact@apnachamber.online. If we delete your information, we may no longer be able to provide the service.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If web analytics are turned on, they record only combined visit data (pages viewed, approximate location, device type), not who you are.

Security

Your data is stored in password-protected accounts, and only we have access. No method of sending data over the internet is completely safe, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Complaints

For any privacy question or complaint, write to contact@apnachamber.online. If we cannot resolve it, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Please note

This policy covers this website and the service described on it. It is not legal advice. The website we build for you will have its own privacy notice, written for your practice.

Terms of Service

Last updated: 13 November 2026

These terms apply to website design, development and content services provided by ApnaChamber. If we give you a separate written quotation or agreement, that document comes first.

What we provide

We provide design, development, copywriting, and domain and email setup for the package you choose. The scope of each package is listed on the pricing page. Anything outside that scope, such as extra pages, logo design, extra languages or WordPress administration, is quoted separately before we start.

What we do not provide

We are not a law firm and we do not give legal advice. We do not certify that your website follows the Bar Council of India Rules or any other rule. We write content to keep it informational, but the final responsibility for the website is the owner's.

We do not promise clients, rankings, traffic or enquiries, and we make no comparisons or claims about results.

Your responsibilities

You will give us correct details (name, enrolment number, State Bar Council, qualifications, practice areas and office address). You will confirm that you have the right to use any photograph or text you give us. You will review the drafts and approve them.

If you take more than seven days to send content or approvals, the project is paused. The delivery timeline restarts when we receive what we need.

Payment

A 50% advance is due when we send the draft for your approval. The balance is due at launch. Prices are in Indian Rupees and do not include taxes, unless stated. From the second year, domain and hosting renewals are charged at cost.

Timeline

We deliver a free sample within 48 hours of receiving your details. After you approve, we aim to launch within five working days. Complex requirements may take longer, and we will tell you about any delay before continuing.

Revisions

Two rounds of revisions are included before launch. After launch, changes are covered by the Care Plan (₹600 per month, with two small changes each month). Without the plan, each change costs ₹500. If we made a mistake, we correct it free of charge at any time.

Ownership

Once you have paid in full, we hand over the website code to you, and the domain and hosting account are registered in your name. While we work together, we hold manager-level access only. We may show the finished website in our portfolio unless you tell us in writing not to. The source code is handed over either through a private GitHub repository, with you added as the owner, or as a code file, whichever you prefer.

Sample websites

Any sample we build before you pay is for you to evaluate. If you decide not to go ahead, you owe nothing and neither side has any further duty.

Liability

Our total liability for any claim about the service is limited to the amount you paid for that project. We are not responsible for indirect or resulting losses, for problems caused by third-party providers, for domain or hosting outages, or for content you supplied.

Governing law

These terms follow the laws of India. Only the courts at Vadodara, Gujarat have jurisdiction.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Free sample stage

The sample website we build before you pay is free. We collect no payment at this stage, and you owe nothing if you decide not to go ahead.

Cancelling before the draft is delivered

If you cancel after paying the advance but before we send the content draft, we refund the full advance within seven working days. We will deduct only any domain registration cost already paid in your name.

Cancelling after the draft is delivered

Once we have sent the draft and build, the work is already done and must be paid for. The advance is not refundable at this stage. The domain, hosting and email set up in your name remain yours.

Cancelling after launch

Payments for work that has been delivered and launched are not refundable. Domain and hosting renewals that have already been paid to third-party providers cannot be refunded once processed, because we pass these charges on at cost.

Care Plan

You can cancel the monthly Care Plan at any time. It ends at the close of the current billing month. If you paid for an annual Care Plan, we refund the unused complete months, minus any domain or hosting renewal we have already paid for you.

If we cancel

If we cannot deliver the agreed work, we will refund the amount you paid for the part not delivered within seven working days. If you prefer, we will complete the work at no extra cost.

If something is not working

If something we built does not work as agreed, tell us and we will fix it free of charge. A refund is not the first solution for a problem that can be fixed.

How to ask for a refund

Write to contact@apnachamber.online from the email address you used for the project. Include your name, project name and payment reference. We reply within three working days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within seven working days.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before you raise a chargeback with your bank. Most problems are solved faster by email.
