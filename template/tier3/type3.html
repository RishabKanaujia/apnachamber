The firm

A chambers, not a directory

Four advocates practising together at Vadodara since 2009, with a single register, a single filing system and one point of contact for every client.

Patel & Associates was started by Nilesh Patel in 2009, the year he was enrolled with the Bar Council of Gujarat. For the first five years it was a single-advocate practice at the District Court, Vadodara.

Rupal Desai joined in 2014, Arjun Mehta in 2017 and Kavita Shah in 2019. What began as shared chambers space became a working firm: one register of matters, one diary of dates, and a rule that any advocate can read any file in the office.

No brief is accepted that the chambers cannot staff properly.

That is a practical rule, not a slogan. Where a matter requires more time than the chambers can give it, or an appearance before a forum where none of the four advocates ordinarily practises, the instruction is declined at the outset.

Work is conducted on a written scope. Fees are recorded before the file is opened. Clients are told the next date, and what is expected to happen on it.

How we work

Three rules

01

One advocate per file

The advocate who takes the first meeting stays on the matter until it is disposed of. Files are not passed around.

02

Written scope, written fee

The work and the fee are recorded before the file is opened. Nothing is added later without being confirmed in writing.

03

Straight advice

Where a matter is weak on the papers, that is said at the first meeting rather than after the filing.

Practice

Areas of practice

Six areas of work accepted by the chambers, and the forums before which its advocates appear.

01 — Civil litigation

Suits and applications concerning money claims, possession and injunctions, conducted through to decree and execution.

  • Recovery suits
  • Injunction applications
  • Partition suits
  • Execution proceedings

02 — Criminal defence

Representation at the stage of investigation, bail and trial, and appeals against conviction before the Sessions Court.

  • Bail applications
  • Sessions trials
  • Criminal appeals
  • Revision applications

03 — Family & matrimonial

Petitions relating to marriage, maintenance and children, filed and contested before the Family Court, Vadodara.

  • Divorce petitions
  • Maintenance
  • Child custody
  • Guardianship

04 — Property & title

Verification of title, drafting and registration of instruments, and disputes concerning possession and tenancy.

  • Title search
  • Sale deeds
  • Partition deeds
  • Tenancy disputes

05 — Consumer protection

Complaints under the consumer protection law, from filing through to the final order and its execution.

  • District Commission
  • State Commission
  • Appeals

06 — Documentation

Drafting of instruments and correspondence, with the formalities for execution explained before signature.

  • Agreements
  • Legal notices
  • Affidavits
  • Power of attorney
