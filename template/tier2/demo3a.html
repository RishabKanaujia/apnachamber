About the Firm

Two Decades of Steady Practice in Gujarat

Patel & Associates was established in 2004 with one chamber opposite the District Court in Vadodara, and a working rule that has not changed since: read the record closely, advise honestly, and prepare every matter as if it will be contested.

The firm now advises individuals, families, builders, traders and companies across Gujarat. Our work runs from title verification and partition suits to matrimonial disputes, commercial recovery, banking matters and regular appearances before the Gujarat High Court.
